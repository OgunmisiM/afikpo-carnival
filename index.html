Maiden Edition: December 2026

The Afikpo International Carnival (AIC) is a week-long cultural festival that showcases Afikpo’s living heritage, performing arts, and eco-tourism attractions.

Organized by Afikpo Carnival & Resorts Ltd in partnership with Federal Ministry of Art, Culture , Tourism and Creative Economy (FMACTCE), World Carnival Commission (WCC), and the United Nations (UN). AIC 2026 will host local and international performers, exhibitors, and cultural ambassadors.

Afikpo International Carnival (AIC) will bring together all the cultural carnivals in Ebonyi State and showcase to the world for international recognition.
